The 4 Ideas, Explained Simply

1. Twin Compatibility Matrix

You visit someone's profile page, type a bit about yourself, and get a score showing how well you'd match with that person — plus what would go well and what might cause friction. Like a personality-match quiz, but for one specific person.


2. Opportunity Feed

Every day, the app finds real job or investor listings from the internet and shows them on your dashboard. Your AI twin writes you a message to reach out to one, even if you're the only person using the app.
